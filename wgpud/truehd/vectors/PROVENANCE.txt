Both are raw TrueHD streams of 48 kHz access units with four substreams: 2-, 6-, and 8-channel presentations and the 16-channel one.
fba_atmos_obj.mlp holds 140 access units; its 16-channel presentation is an object presentation of one LFE bed channel and 15 dynamic objects, all silent,
whose object audio metadata arrives in the Evolution frames of access units 0, 38, 76, and 115.
fba_atmos_cbi.mlp holds 136 access units; its 16-channel presentation is a channel bed without object metadata,
L R C LFE Ls Rs Lb Rb Tfl Tfr Tsl Tsr Tbl Tbr Lw Rw, and its 8-channel presentation is 7.1.

single-substream-5.1.thd is ours: a raw TrueHD stream of 300 48 kHz access units of 40 samples, 5.1(side) in one substream,
the layout FFmpeg's decoder refuses (every restart header fails "Assignment of matrix channel 0 to invalid output channel").
FFmpeg's TrueHD encoder writes a multichannel stream as two substreams, so it was encoded by FFmpeg a59498db085e3d635532397128550141ab87408a,
the engine's pinned FFmpeg, built with Emscripten with only the TrueHD encoder and decoder, after three changes to libavcodec/mlpenc.c:

  -        ctx->num_substreams = 1 + (avctx->ch_layout.nb_channels > 2);
  +        ctx->num_substreams = 1;
  -            ctx->thd_substream_info  = 0x3C;
  +            ctx->thd_substream_info  = 0x04;
  -        rh->max_channel        = FFMIN(avctx->ch_layout.nb_channels, 2) - 1;
  +        rh->max_channel        = avctx->ch_layout.nb_channels - 1;

so its substream 0 carries all six channels and the major sync names it as the 6-channel presentation's only substream.
The input is 12000 samples per channel of S32P at 24 bits, a sine at a quarter of full scale per channel, rounded with lrint:
440 Hz on FL, 554 Hz on FR, 660 Hz on FC, 55 Hz on LFE, 784 Hz on SL, and 988 Hz on SR, sample n of frequency f being sin(2 pi f n / 48000).
Its 24-bit samples, interleaved FL FR FC LFE SL SR as little-endian int32, have the SHA-256 the package test checks its decode against,
which the stream's own lossless checks agree with.
