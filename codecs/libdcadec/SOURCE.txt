Corresponding source: each WebGPU Player release attaches the exact dcadec tree
(dcadec-b93deed1a231dd6dd7e39b9fe7d2abe05aa00158.tar.gz) and the engine's codec
sources, both written by "make -C codecs source-archives".

To rebuild, or to relink against a modified libdcadec, run from the engine root
with GNU Make, Git, and a POSIX shell (Git Bash on Windows):

    make -C codecs sources libdcadec EMSDK=/path/to/emsdk-4.0.13
