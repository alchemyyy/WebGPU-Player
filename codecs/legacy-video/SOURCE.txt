FFmpeg MPEG-2 Video and VC-1 decoder (legacy-video-decode.js and .wasm)

This WebAssembly module combines FFmpeg, licensed under the GNU Lesser General
Public License version 2.1 or later (LICENSE.ffmpeg.txt), with the MIT-licensed
bridge bridge.c (LICENSE.bridge.txt).

Upstream source:  FFmpeg commit a59498db085e3d635532397128550141ab87408a
                  https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/tree/a59498db085e3d635532397128550141ab87408a
Toolchain:        Emscripten 4.0.13 (2659582941bef14008476903f48941909db1b196)
Build recipe:     the legacy-video target of codecs/Makefile in
                  https://github.com/alchemyyy/WebGPU-Player
                  (LEGACY_VIDEO_* variables)

Corresponding source: each WebGPU Player release attaches the exact FFmpeg tree
(ffmpeg-a59498db085e3d635532397128550141ab87408a.tar.gz) and the engine's codec
sources, both written by "make -C codecs source-archives".

To rebuild, or to relink against a modified FFmpeg, run from the engine root
with GNU Make, Git, and a POSIX shell (Git Bash on Windows):

    make -C codecs sources legacy-video EMSDK=/path/to/emsdk-4.0.13
