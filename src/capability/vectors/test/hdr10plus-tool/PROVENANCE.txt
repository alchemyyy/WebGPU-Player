dhdr10-opt.hevc is an Annex B HEVC Main 10 stream of 30 pictures that x265 encoded with --dhdr10-opt.
Its HDR10+ metadata is profile A: one window, scene statistics, no Bezier curve, and a targeted display of 0.
x265 writes the ST 2094-40 SEI only on the IDR picture and on a picture whose metadata differs from the picture encoded before it, so 18 of the 30 access units carry none.

metadata-dhdr10-opt.json is what hdr10plus_tool extract writes for that stream, and the known answer of the tool's own dhdr10_opt_gaps test.
It lists every picture in display order.
A picture without an SEI takes the metadata of the last SEI before it in decode order, as in FFmpeg.
